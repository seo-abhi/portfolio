About Me

My SEO work usually starts with one question:

What is stopping this website from growing?

From there, I look at the full picture: content, technical health, keyword targeting, competitors, backlinks, Local SEO, and search performance to identify what is holding the website back and where the strongest opportunities are.

I’ve worked on both new websites that needed an SEO foundation from scratch and established websites that needed stronger content, technical performance, search visibility, or backlink profiles.

My work covers both strategy and execution, from identifying the problem and building the plan to coordinating improvements and tracking how they perform over time.

What I enjoy most is turning complex SEO problems into clear, practical action plans and improving them as the data changes.

What I Do

01

On-Page SEO

I optimize content, headings, metadata, internal linking, keyword targeting, and page structure to improve relevance and organic search visibility.

02

Technical SEO

I audit crawlability, indexing, sitemaps, canonicalization, redirects, page speed, and other technical issues, then coordinate the fixes needed to build a stronger search foundation.

03

Content & Keyword Strategy

I use keyword research, search intent, competitor analysis, and content planning to identify which pages should be created, improved, or prioritized for organic growth.

04

Off-Page SEO

I build relevant backlinks, analyze competitor link profiles, and plan target pages and anchor text to strengthen the backlink profile and support search visibility.

05

Local SEO

I improve local search visibility through Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword targeting, location-focused pages, and stronger local relevance.

06

AI Search Optimization

I improve content clarity, structure, technical accessibility, and topical relevance to support visibility across Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, and other AI-driven search experiences.

My SEO Process

1. Audit

Understand the website’s current SEO health, technical issues, content gaps, indexing, and search performance.

2. Research

Study keywords, competitors, search intent, backlinks, and existing performance to identify the strongest growth opportunities.

3. Strategy

Prioritize the highest-impact opportunities and turn the findings into a clear, practical SEO action plan.

4. Execution

Implement and coordinate improvements across content, on-page SEO, technical SEO, internal linking, backlinks, and Local SEO.

5. Track & Improve

Monitor rankings, clicks, impressions, traffic, indexing, and conversions to understand what is working and continuously refine the strategy.
